# IoT Devices - Now Accessible!

## ✅ FIXED: IoT Device Pairing Now Available

The IoT device pairing screens are now accessible from the Health tab!

---

## 📱 How to Access IoT Devices

### Method 1: From Health Tab (NEW!)
1. Open app
2. Tap "Health" in bottom navigation
3. See "IoT Health Devices" section at top
4. Tap "Add Device" button → Opens device pairing intro
5. Or tap any device card → Opens device management

### Method 2: Direct Navigation
- All IoT screens are accessible via routes:
  - `/iot/device-pairing-intro` - Start pairing process
  - `/iot/device-management` - Manage connected devices
  - `/iot/device-pairing-bluetooth` - Bluetooth pairing
  - `/iot/device-pairing-wifi` - WiFi setup
  - And 11 more IoT screens...
