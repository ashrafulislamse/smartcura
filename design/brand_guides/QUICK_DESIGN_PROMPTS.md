## 📱 APP ICON PROMPTS (3 Different Apps)

### PATIENT APP ICON ⭐ (User's Custom Design - Score: 99/100)

```
Create a professional healthcare app icon monogram for "SmartCura".

Design concept:
- Stylized capital "S" (left) and "C" (right) forming a single, unified heart outline
- The letters themselves create the heart shape (no separate heart outline)
- One continuous ECG/heartbeat line passes horizontally through the center and visually merges with the letters

Geometry & precision (CRITICAL):
- ALL strokes must have EXACTLY the same thickness (heart outline, S, C, ECG)
- Stroke weight: 8-10% of icon width (optimal for scalability)
- Smooth, open Bézier curves only — no tight curls or decorative loops
- Perfect left–right symmetry
- Soft, rounded heart bottom (no sharp point)
- Simplified geometry suitable for medical branding
- Clear negative space inside heart shape

Style:
- Flat vector design (corporate logo quality)
- Ultra-clean, minimal, medical-grade
- Calm and professional (not playful)
- No shadows, gradients, glow, texture, or 3D effects
- Must remain clear and readable at 16px
- Professional logo design standards (not artistic/hand-drawn)

Colors:
- Medical blue (#2563EB) for S, C, and heart strokes
- Cyan (#00E5FF) for ECG line only
- No additional colors
- Color space: sRGB

Canvas:
- 1024 × 1024 px
- Transparent background (PNG with alpha channel, 32-bit)
- Icon centered with ~10% padding (safe zone: 922×922px active area)
- NO text, NO borders, NO rounded corners (OS applies these)

Overall feel:
- Trustworthy healthcare technology
- Modern, calm, and professional
- App Store–ready for iOS and Android
- Must look human-designed with precise geometric construction
- Corporate identity quality, not AI-art or decorative

Target: Patient app icon for SmartCura healthcare platform
```
